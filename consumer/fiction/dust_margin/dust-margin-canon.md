# The Dust Margin — Canon Graph

**Part One complete — state as of Scene 41.** Companion to `the-dust-margin.md`. Built on the Forge Stack (`forge-stack/governance-v1`) — see `docs/gate-pattern.md` and `docs/datacube.md` for the mechanics this graph follows.

**Standing ingress rule (adopted mid-arc, in force from Scene 27 onward):** any claim attributed to a speaker via dialogue or reported speech defaults to OPINION (self-reported), COUNTER placeholder open — no exceptions for dramatic momentum, sympathetic speakers, or narrative pressure. FACT is reserved for the narration's own assertions, a character's own stated preference/decision (self-authenticating — you can't be wrong about what you want), or claims independently corroborated by something already in the graph. Two untrusted sources agreeing with each other does not manufacture FACT.

Lens legend: **FACT** verifiable on the page · **COUNTER** contradicting material · **OPINION** a character's stated view, self-reported claims default here · **FICTION** an in-story untruth, confirmed · **CONTEXT** situational framing · **UNKNOWN** deliberately unresolved.

A claim only earns FACT status when the text confirms it independently of the speaker's own word. Self-reports — even from sympathetic characters — start in OPINION with a COUNTER placeholder open, no exceptions.

## The Dust Margin (universe seed)

**Tone:** dusty and desperate, where hope's a fuel you ration.

**Hard rules (λ 2.0, Tier 5 to override):**
- No FTL. News and travel move at ship-speed.
- Death is final. No resurrection, no uploads.
- Water is the scarcity. Ship range is measured in water-tank days.
- The frontier is a state, not a map — "the space between," navigation dangerous, communication unreliable.

- Rena's Scene Three lie ("I don't know what it is") — confirmed FICTION, retconned, established her core pattern: withholds until forced. **(S3/S5)**
- Kael's contradictory confessions — S25's active account accepted as canon, S21 retconned as the lie. **(S21/S25, resolved S33)**
- Corvus's claim that Rena was aboard The Last Light — graduated OPINION → FACT via direct corroboration. **(S30, confirmed S33)**
