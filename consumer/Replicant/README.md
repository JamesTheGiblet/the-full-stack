# 🧬 Replicant

**A hybrid bio-inspired swarm framework, built on the Forge Stack.**

*Born pregnant. Born ready. Born signed.*

> **Status: v1.0 — built, tested, running.** Python and Rust are parallel implementations, not a prototype and a port. Neither is authoritative; where they differ, [`feature-parity-spec.md`](feature-parity-spec.md) is the record of which is ahead.

Replicant is a **consumer** of the Forge Stack. It holds its own capsule namespace, its own consumer ledger anchored to the root chain, and inherits governance under **MSL-1.0** via the `inherits` field of every capsule it mints.

This document covers the project as a whole. For implementation detail, follow the links in [Structure](#-structure).

---

## 🎯 The actual question

Replicant cherry-picks mechanisms from ants, bees, termites, spiders, wasps, mole-rats and aphids, and asks a specific question: **what happens when a stigmergic swarm can pay energy to make more of itself — and every claim it makes is classified, scored and witnessed?**

Most swarm frameworks fix the population and study coordination. Replicant makes population a decision variable and studies the trade-off.

An agent that spends 40 energy to make a child is an agent that isn't foraging. A swarm that doubles is a swarm that halves its per-agent energy reserve. The interesting result is not "the swarm grows" — that's trivial. It's **where growth stops on its own**, and whether that ceiling tracks the environment's energy throughput.

The second question is the one the stack makes askable: **a pheromone trail is a claim, not a fact.** Classical stigmergy treats a deposit as ground truth. It isn't. It's one agent's assertion, possibly stale, possibly wrong, possibly hostile. Replicant runs the whole signalling layer through the Forge Stack spine — Declare → Classify → Trust-score → Audit → Act — so the swarm's belief state is inspectable rather than implied.

**Both questions now have answers.** Population self-regulates to ~7 agents from an initial 10 without a cap being hit. Health stabilises at ~0.79 across seasons and seeds. And the swarm detects fabricated trails by finding no food where a claim said there would be some — no oracle, no label.

Seven mechanisms in isolation is a zoo, not an architecture. The couplings are the design — and the spine is what couples them.

## 🔩 The Forge Stack spine

| Stage | System → artefact | In Replicant |
|-------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Declare** | SCP → `sc` | Every agent is born with a signed genome capsule. `inherits` records the parent's `scp_id`. A child is a new identity, not a revision. |
| **Classify** | DataCube → `cube` | Every deposit and broadcast enters the store under a lens: FACT, COUNTER, OPINION, CONTEXT, UNKNOWN. |
| **Trust-score** | Leighton Weight Engine → λ | Each agent holds a λ derived from an append-only event ledger. Computed on read, never stored. |
| **Audit** | ChronoSCRIBE → ledger | Births, deaths, attestations and seals are hash-chained events, anchored to root. |
| **Act** | HAL → seal | Escalating actions require λ above a tier threshold; the seal refuses to issue below it. Deploying to physical hardware sits at the top tier — see [`esp32-robotics-extension-spec.md`](esp32-robotics-extension-spec.md) for what that deployment actually looks like. |

### What v1.0 settled

- **λ is an event ledger, not a value.** Computed on read from an append-only stream. The earlier design kept a two-field cache and a `verify_cache` step; both are gone, and with them the entire class of cache-vs-ledger mismatch bugs. There is no cache to mismatch.
- **No FICTION label.** Adversary claims are structurally identical to honest ones. The swarm judges by outcome, not by label — a verifier checks the environment and finds nothing where a claim promised food. Detection is organic; there is no oracle.
- **Recidivism is priced.** Repeated offences escalate penalty magnitude by 100% per prior offence. One isolated mistake costs −0.08; three proven lies land at λ≈0.58, below quarantine. Honest agents are not condemned for a single error.
- **Rogue status is derived, not latched.** Quarantine below **0.60**, expulsion below **0.15**, both computed from the ledger. A quarantined agent recovers by stopping — validated at 0.410 → 0.602.
- **Consequences belong to the world.** Penalties are applied when claims are adjudicated false, never by agents themselves. Credulity costs −0.05; scepticism earns +0.03.

### The diagnostic that matters

**An empty COUNTER bin means disconfirmation was never sought.** A swarm whose COUNTER bin stays empty is not always right — it never checks. In current runs COUNTER tracks claims closely at every scale, which is the mechanism working rather than a coincidence.

## ⚙️ The tick contract

State is double-buffered. Each tick reads a frozen world and writes a new one.

```
Tick(N) → Tick(N+1)

  Phase 1  SENSE      parallel, read-only     world[N] → percepts
  Phase 2  DECIDE     parallel, pure          percepts → Vec<Intent>
  Phase 3  RESOLVE    single-threaded         intents  → world[N+1]
  Phase 4  CLASSIFY   single-threaded         claims   → cube store
  Phase 5  SCORE      parallel, disjoint      attestations → λ update
  Phase 6  WITNESS    single-threaded         Merkle root → one signed ledger row
  Phase 7  DECAY      parallel, disjoint      field + λ decay
```

Nothing writes to another agent's fields. A waggle dance is an `Intent::Broadcast`, resolved in phase 3 and classified in phase 4. Conflict rules are explicit and total, or determinism dies: two agents claiming one resource resolve by lower `AgentId`.

Same seed → identical ledger head hash. Not "the final state looks the same" — cryptographic equality over the entire causal history.

## 🔋 Energy conservation

Energy is the only currency and does not appear from nowhere. Movement, sensing, broadcast radius, attestation and birth overhead all draw on it; replication is a transfer with loss.

**Attestation costs energy, and that is the load-bearing decision in the design.** Free checking means everyone checks everything and the COUNTER bin fills trivially. Expensive checking means nobody checks and the swarm runs on unverified OPINION until a poisoned trail starves it. Somewhere between is a swarm that allocates scepticism efficiently — finding that band is the experiment.

`max_agents` is a diagnostic, not a design parameter. Hitting the cap means the environment is too rich or the costs too cheap.

## 📜 Governance

Replicant inherits governance under **MSL-1.0** via the Forge Stack. The `inherits` field of every capsule records the governance in force at signing — historical, not a live pointer. Superseding governance does not invalidate prior declarations.

**Single-operator disclosure:** this project runs in a stack without multiple independent validators. Every seal carries `separation = "none"`, meaning it records deliberation and intent, not enforced separation of duties. It becomes `verified` only when a distinct identity issues the authoriser score. Claiming otherwise would be the exact failure mode the stack exists to prevent.
